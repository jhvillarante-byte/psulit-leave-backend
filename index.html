Run Payroll

Base pay is half the fixed monthly salary. Recorded absences, unpaid leave, and unworked special holidays are deducted at the daily rate and reduce net pay (monthly salary ÷ divisor). Worked regular holidays add 100% of the daily rate, worked special holidays add 30% — holiday dates for 2026 are built in automatically per Proclamation No. 1006, and whether an employee "worked" a holiday is detected from their Time Clock kiosk check-ins. Approved leaves from the Leave Request app are also detected automatically: Sick and Vacation leave draw down the employee's paid leave credit balance (deducted when you finalize this run); Emergency/Other leave is treated as unpaid and docked at the daily rate. Night shift differential (10% of hourly rate for paid hours between 10 PM–6 AM) is added for every worked day on a night-inclusive shift; the standard 1-hour unpaid meal break is excluded from a 9+ hour shift. Reimbursements entered on the employee record are added separately to net pay and are not included in taxable gross. Tardiness is not deducted from payroll. Additional regular hours are entered and approved manually; early arrival and late departure are not automatically paid. SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG are computed on the actual fixed monthly salary and split evenly across both cutoffs; withholding tax uses the BIR semi-monthly TRAIN Law table. These are simplified calculations — verify against official schedules and your own holiday-pay policy before remitting.

Extra Holiday Dates

The 2026 official regular and special holidays (Proclamation No. 1006) are already built in automatically. Finalizing permanently saves this run's numbers (gross, deductions, tax, net) and subtracts loan installments from each employee's remaining balance. Only do this once per cutoff — re-finalizing the same cutoff will ask you to confirm to avoid double-deducting. Use "Load Saved Record" any time afterward to see exactly what was finalized, even if attendance is edited later. "Download Bank Disbursement PDF" produces a PDF with each employee's name, branch, BDO account number, and net pay for uploading to your bank's batch transfer portal. "Download Remittance Summary" produces a PDF report, grouped by agency (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR), listing each employee's ID number alongside their Employee Share, Employer Share, and combined Total to remit — with a subtotal per agency (BIR shows Employee Share only, since withholding tax has no employer counterpart). "Download BAFI Remittance PDF" produces a PDF listing everyone with an active BAFI Loan deduction this cutoff, alongside the amount to remit and remaining balance.
