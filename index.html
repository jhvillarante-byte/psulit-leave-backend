Run Payroll

Base pay is half the fixed monthly salary. Absences, unpaid leave, and unworked special holidays are deducted at the daily rate (monthly salary ÷ divisor). Worked regular holidays add 100% of the daily rate, worked special holidays add 30% — holiday dates for 2026 are built in automatically per Proclamation No. 1006, and whether an employee "worked" a holiday is detected from their Time Clock kiosk check-ins. Approved leaves from the Leave Request app are also detected automatically: Sick and Vacation leave draw down the employee's paid leave credit balance (deducted when you finalize this run); Emergency/Other leave is treated as unpaid and docked at the daily rate. Night shift differential (10% of hourly rate for hours between 10 PM–6 AM) is added for every worked day on a night-inclusive shift. Tardiness is deducted per minute late beyond the grace period, based on Time Clock kiosk check-ins compared to the employee's scheduled shift start — this only applies to days with a matching kiosk check-in record. SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG are computed on the actual fixed monthly salary and split evenly across both cutoffs; withholding tax uses the BIR semi-monthly TRAIN Law table. These are simplified calculations — verify against official schedules and your own holiday-pay policy before remitting.

Extra Holiday Dates

The 2026 official regular and special holidays (Proclamation No. 1006) are already built in automatically. Use this only to add dates not on that list — e.g. Eid'l Fitr / Eid'l Adha once announced, or an LGU-specific special non-working day for one of your branches. One per line, format: YYYY-MM-DD, Name, regular or YYYY-MM-DD, Name, special.
